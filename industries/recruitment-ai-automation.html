AI Automation for Recruitment Agencies

AI Automation for Recruitment Agencies in Norwich and East Anglia

A vacancy comes in on a Tuesday morning. By Wednesday a rival agency has already put three candidates forward and yours is still waiting on a callback. Recruitment is won and lost in the gaps between conversations, and most agencies lose time in the same three places: acknowledging candidates, chasing missing information and updating the CRM after the fact.

Cambridge and the wider tech corridor into Norwich generate a steady stream of specialist roles that need filling fast, alongside the everyday hiring every business runs. We build workflows that handle the repeatable steps around candidate and client engagement, so your consultants spend their time on the calls that actually place people.

What this looks like in practice

  • Turns a vacancy enquiry into a structured brief and a task for the right consultant.
  • Acknowledges new applications and asks for whatever information is still missing.
  • Summarises CVs against agreed criteria, ready for a consultant to review.
  • Runs personalised follow-up sequences for candidates, clients and contacts gone quiet.
  • Books interviews and sends reminders, confirmations and next steps.
  • Updates CRM records after calls, emails, meetings and form submissions.

Benefits

  • Faster replies to candidates and clients, including outside office hours.
  • Less admin standing between a consultant and their next call.
  • A more consistent candidate journey without losing the personal touch.
  • A cleaner, more current pipeline with far less manual updating.

Frequently asked questions

Will candidates realise they're dealing with an automated system?

For the routine steps, yes, and that's fine. Most candidates prefer an instant acknowledgement to silence. Anything that needs a real conversation, a screening call, an offer, a difficult update, stays with your consultants.

Can this work alongside our existing CRM?

Yes. We connect to what you already run, Bullhorn, Vincere, JobAdder or another system, rather than asking you to migrate your data or change how consultants work day to day.

What's the first workflow most agencies automate?

Usually candidate acknowledgement and CRM updates, because they're high volume, low judgement and cost the most time when done by hand. We start there and expand once it's proven.

Do you work with recruitment agencies outside Cambridge?

Yes. We're based in Norwich and work with agencies across Norfolk, Suffolk, Cambridgeshire and Essex, covering everything from single-desk recruiters to multi-branch agencies.
