AI Automation for Logistics & Transport

AI Automation for Logistics & Transport Teams in Norwich and East Anglia

Felixstowe, on the Suffolk coast, is the UK's busiest container port, handling around four in ten containers that arrive in the country. A huge amount of what moves through it ends up on a lorry heading through Ipswich, Norwich or Colchester, tracked by a small operations team on the phone and in their inbox. When a delivery slips, that team often finds out from an angry customer before they find out from their own system.

We build AI workflows that turn scattered updates, booking emails, status checks, exception alerts, into organised, visible actions. The result is a calmer operation, customers who hear about a delay before they have to ask, and a team with time to actually solve problems instead of chasing them.

What this looks like in practice

  • Sorts inbound booking, quote and delivery enquiries by urgency, location or service type.
  • Pulls key details out of order emails and documents into a structured workflow.
  • Drafts and sends routine milestone updates to customers and partners.
  • Spots a delivery exception in an incoming message and alerts the right person straight away.
  • Creates reminders for collection, delivery, proof of delivery and document follow-up.
  • Produces a daily operational summary from your connected systems and team updates.

Benefits

  • Far less time spent monitoring inboxes and chasing status by hand.
  • Customers hear about delays before they have to ask.
  • Exceptions reach the right person before they turn into a bigger problem.
  • A clearer daily view of what actually needs attention.

Frequently asked questions

Can this connect to our existing TMS or WMS?

In most cases, yes. We work with what you already run and build the connections your specific setup needs, rather than asking you to switch systems.

What happens when something goes wrong with a delivery?

The system flags the exception and routes it to whoever should see it first, with the relevant details attached, rather than leaving it buried in an inbox until someone notices.

Is this only for larger logistics operations?

No. Smaller operators often see the fastest payback, because a two or three person team simply can't monitor every inbox and phone line at once. The automation covers the gaps a bigger team would staff around.

Do you work with logistics businesses across East Anglia, or just around Felixstowe?

Across the whole region. We're based in Norwich and work with operators throughout Norfolk, Suffolk, Cambridgeshire and Essex, including businesses moving goods through Felixstowe, Ipswich and the wider port corridor.
