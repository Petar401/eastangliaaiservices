AI Automation for Estate Agents

AI Automation for Estate Agents in Norwich and East Anglia

A property enquiry in Norwich on a Saturday afternoon has a short shelf life. Wait until Monday to reply and there's a good chance the buyer has already booked three other viewings and forgotten which agency this one even was. Agencies don't lose instructions and sales because they did a bad job, they lose them because someone else replied first.

We build AI systems that keep the first response and the routine follow-up moving around the clock, so your team has more time for viewings, valuations and the relationships that actually win instructions. Applicants get looked after between enquiry and viewing, vendors and landlords get kept in the loop, and your negotiators stay at the centre of every decision that matters.

What this looks like in practice

  • Replies to a new property enquiry promptly and collects what your team actually needs.
  • Qualifies buyer, tenant, vendor and landlord enquiries through a short set of guided questions.
  • Offers viewing slots, sends confirmations and follows up with a reminder.
  • Matches applicants to relevant properties against agreed criteria.
  • Triggers follow-up after a viewing, a valuation or a new instruction.
  • Updates CRM records and assigns the right next action automatically.

Benefits

  • Every lead gets a faster response, including outside office hours.
  • Applicants stay engaged between the first enquiry and the viewing.
  • Less repetitive admin for negotiators and branch teams.
  • A more consistent, professional experience at every stage.

Frequently asked questions

Will an automated response put buyers or sellers off?

Most people would rather get an instant, useful reply than wait until Monday. The automation handles the first response and the routine follow-up, and your negotiators step in for anything that needs a real conversation, a valuation, an offer, a difficult vendor call.

Can this connect to our existing CRM and property portals?

Yes. We connect to what you already run, Reapit, Jupix, Street or another system, and to the portals you list on, rather than asking you to change how the branch works.

Does this work for lettings as well as sales?

Yes. The same enquiry-qualify-follow-up pattern applies to tenants and landlords, and tends to matter even more for lettings, where response speed has a direct effect on void periods.

Do you only work with agencies in Norwich, or across the wider region?

Across the region. We're based in Norwich and work with agencies throughout Norfolk, Suffolk, Cambridgeshire and Essex, from single branches to multi-office operations.
