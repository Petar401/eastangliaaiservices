What this looks like in practice

  • Pulls the key details off invoices and supporting documents, then routes them for review or approval.
  • Reads incoming client emails, sorts them by type and creates the right follow-up task automatically.
  • Sends a timely, polite request when a document, record or approval is missing.
  • Drafts routine status summaries from information already sitting in your systems.
  • Keeps client and job records aligned across your CRM, inbox and workflow tools.
  • Flags anything incomplete or unusual for a person to check, rather than guessing.

Benefits

  • Less manual data entry and fewer repeat chases for the same document.
  • A routine process that is consistent enough to actually track.
  • More time for accountants and finance staff to do client-facing, judgement-led work.
  • A cleaner hand-off between clients, administrators and the finance team.

Frequently asked questions

How is this different from the automation already built into Xero or Sage?

Built-in automation stops at the edge of one piece of software. We connect what happens before and after it, the inbox, the client portal, the approval chain, so information moves between systems instead of getting rekeyed at each step.

Will an AI system have access to sensitive client financial data?

Only what the workflow needs, and only within the permissions you set. We scope data access per task, keep a clear audit trail, and your team keeps sign-off on anything that touches client money or advice.

How long does this take to set up for a small practice?

Most practices in Norwich and the wider region are running their first automated workflow within a few weeks, starting with the single process costing the most admin time, then expanding from there.

Do you work with accounting firms outside Norwich?

Yes. We're based in Norwich and work with practices across Norfolk, Suffolk, Cambridgeshire and Essex, on site or fully remote, wherever your team is.
