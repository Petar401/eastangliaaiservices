About the Calculator

How the calculator works

It adds up three costs that most small businesses never see on a report, each over a 48-week working year:

  • Missed calls: calls per week × % missed × your conversion rate × average customer value.
  • Slow lead replies: leads per week × % answered after an hour or more × your conversion rate × average customer value, counted at half value, because a slow reply loses some of those leads, not all of them.
  • Manual admin: admin hours per week × your fully-loaded hourly staff cost.

Every figure comes from the numbers you enter. The tool doesn't add hidden multipliers, and results are rounded so they don't look more precise than they are.

Who it's for

It's most useful for businesses that rely on phone calls and enquiries: trades, clinics, salons, estate and letting agents, garages, hospitality and professional services. It also works for any team that spends hours a week re-typing data, chasing paperwork or answering the same questions.

How to use the result

Treat the total as a ballpark. Use it to decide which problem is worth fixing first, not as an accounting figure. If missed calls are the biggest cost, an AI receptionist or voice agent that answers 24/7 is usually the fastest payback. If slow replies are the biggest cost, instant automated lead replies help most. If admin is, workflow automation does. Compare the yearly loss with the cost of a fix, and you have a simple payback period. Not sure your business is ready? Take the AI Readiness Assessment or book a free strategy call.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is my annual business loss actually calculated?

We combine three things you tell us — how many calls and leads you get, how many you miss or respond to slowly, and how much manual admin costs in staff time — with conservative, industry-standard assumptions, including a 50% lower conversion rate for leads answered after an hour. The result is a categorised estimate designed to show where the biggest gaps are, not a formal audit.

How much do UK small businesses typically lose to missed calls?

Small businesses commonly miss 20-30% of incoming calls, and unanswered calls rarely call back — most go straight to a competitor. For a business getting even 40 calls a week, that can mean losing several thousand pounds a year in unconverted enquiries alone.

Why does response speed to leads matter so much?

Research into lead response times consistently shows conversion odds fall sharply once a lead has waited more than an hour for a reply — often by half or more. An AI chatbot or voice receptionist that responds instantly, day or night, directly closes that gap.

What can I actually do to reduce these losses?

The three biggest levers are: never missing a call (an AI voice receptionist answers every time), responding to leads within minutes instead of hours (an AI chatbot or automated follow-up), and automating the manual admin that eats staff hours.
