The people behind East Anglia AI Services.

East Anglia AI Services is a Norwich-based AI automation consultancy founded in 2026 by Petar Petkov and Magdalena Peeva. We help small and medium businesses across Norfolk, Suffolk, Cambridgeshire and Essex hand repetitive admin, missed calls and manual data work to AI systems built around how they already operate.

Why I started East Anglia AI Services

Before starting the business, I spent more than three years in data administration roles across logistics and warehousing, healthcare and care, retail and e-commerce, and finance and back-office teams. The sectors were different, but the work looked the same everywhere: the same figures typed into two systems, the same reports rebuilt by hand every month, the same enquiries answered one at a time.

I could see how much of that time AI and automation could give back, and how rarely small firms got any help to make it happen. The big consultancies don't work at that size, and off-the-shelf tools rarely fit how a local business actually runs. East Anglia AI Services exists to close that gap: practical systems for Norfolk, Suffolk, Cambridgeshire and Essex businesses, built by someone who has done the manual version of the job himself.

  • Business management and entrepreneurship: currently studying in the UK, applied daily to running and growing the company.
  • Data administration: 3+ years across logistics and warehousing, healthcare and care, retail and e-commerce, and finance and back-office operations.
  • Languages: English, Bulgarian, Serbian, Macedonian, Croatian, Bosnian and Slovenian, plus some German. Useful for businesses whose staff or customers speak these languages, and for multilingual AI assistants and voice agents.
  • Based in: Norwich, Norfolk, and working on-site across East Anglia.

How we work

  • Problem first, tool second. We start with the job that costs you the most time or money, then choose the AI that fits it, not the other way round.
  • Fixed prices, agreed upfront. You get a written quote before any work starts. Our starting prices are published.
  • Built around your tools. We connect what you already use, such as Xero, HubSpot, Outlook, Google Sheets and Slack, rather than asking you to switch.
  • Safe by default. Every system respects UK GDPR, keeps a person in the loop where it matters, and comes with clear rules for your team. Our free AI Use Policy Generator is a good place to start.
  • Local and on-site. We'd rather visit your office in Norwich, Ipswich, Cambridge or Colchester than send a slide deck.
