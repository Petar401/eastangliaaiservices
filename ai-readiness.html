About the Assessment

What the AI Readiness Assessment measures

The assessment scores your business on the ten things that decide whether an AI or automation project pays off: clear goals, where your data lives, which repetitive tasks you've already spotted, how well your tools connect, how your team takes to new software, whether your processes are written down, current AI use, leadership support, budget, and UK GDPR confidence. Each answer is worth 0 to 3 points, so the total is out of 30.

How to read your score

  • 0–9, AI Explorer: start with foundations (goals, data, written processes) before buying any AI tool.
  • 10–17, AI Builder: there are real gaps, but also quick wins. One well-chosen automation is the usual first step.
  • 18–24, AI Advancer: the basics are in place. The work is picking the highest-return targets.
  • 25–30, AI Accelerator: you're ready for custom AI agents and predictive systems. Governance becomes the priority.

Who it's for

It's written for owners and managers of small and medium-sized businesses who want to know whether AI is worth looking at now or later. You don't need any technical knowledge; every question is about how your business runs today.

What to do with the result

The lowest-scoring areas in your report are where to start, because they're what usually holds an AI project back. If you score low on data or processes, fixing those first will make any later automation cheaper. If you score high, pick one costly, repetitive task and automate it. The Business Loss Calculator helps you put a £ figure on it, and if staff already use ChatGPT or Copilot, the AI Use Policy Generator gives them clear rules. You can also book a free strategy call to go through your report with us.
