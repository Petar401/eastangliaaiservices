Suffolk's economy, and where AI fits

Suffolk is home to more than 31,000 businesses and a £21 billion economy, and the Port of Felixstowe handles 36% of UK container trade (Suffolk County Council, 2025). Around Ipswich there is a long-established cluster of insurance, finance and legal firms (Ipswich Borough Council, 2023), and BT's research headquarters at Adastral Park anchors a cluster of more than 150 high-tech companies at Innovation Martlesham (Suffolk Economy, 2025). Advanced manufacturing alone employs 39,200 people across more than 3,000 Suffolk businesses (Suffolk Economy, 2025).

Most of those businesses are small. They don't need an AI lab; they need the admin, the phone and the paperwork to stop eating the day. That is what we build.
