Why work with an AI consultant who lives in Norwich?

Most AI advice is written for large companies in London. Norwich businesses are mostly small and medium firms where the owner still answers the phone, chases invoices and copies figures between systems. We work at that scale: one problem at a time, on-site when it helps, with someone you can actually meet.

Because we are based in Eaton, a meeting in the Golden Triangle, at an industrial estate in Hellesdon or at a shop in Wymondham is a short trip, not a day out. If something needs a look after it goes live, we can come and see it in person instead of trying to fix it over a screen share.

Norwich's economy, and where AI fits

Norwich City Council's economic plan describes a city centre that is increasingly a hub for new and early-stage businesses, with strength in fintech and digital services, alongside Norwich Research Park, the largest concentration of food and health research in Europe (Norwich City Council, 2025).

The city has also been a home of insurance for more than two centuries. Norwich Union was founded here in 1797 and is now part of Aviva (Aviva, n.d.). Brokers, accountants, solicitors and claims specialists still spend much of the day on forms, documents and email, and that is the kind of work today's AI handles well, with a person checking what goes out.

Business groups and funding in Norfolk

You don't have to work out AI on your own. Norfolk Chambers of Commerce is a not-for-profit membership body based in Norwich, and its members can book desks and meeting space at its Business Hub in the city centre (Norfolk Chambers of Commerce, n.d.). For free, impartial advice on growing a business, the New Anglia Growth Hub is the first call.

Money is sometimes available too. Norfolk County Council's Business Growth Fund offers grants of £5,000 to £50,000 towards up to 30% of eligible growth projects, and the Made Smarter programme supports SMEs adopting digital technology, including match-funded grants of up to £20,000 (Norfolk County Council, 2025; New Anglia Growth Hub, 2025). Rounds open and close, so ask the Growth Hub what is live before you plan around it. We can help you write up an AI project so it fits the criteria.

What working together looks like

It starts with a free visit. We sit with whoever does the work, not just the owner, and list the jobs that eat the most hours. You then get a written, fixed-price quote and a plain-English plan, and nothing happens until you agree to it. We build around what you already use, test it on real work, then keep an eye on it after go-live. Most Norwich firms begin with one of three things: answering missed calls, processing documents and invoices, or a half-day of AI training for a team that already uses ChatGPT or Copilot.

Not sure which comes first? The free AI Readiness Assessment takes two minutes, and the Business Loss Calculator puts a pound figure on missed calls and admin. You can also read how the right AI helps East Anglia's small businesses grow.

Frequently asked questions

Can we meet you in person in Norwich?

Yes. We live and work in Eaton, Norwich, so we can meet at your premises anywhere in the city at a time that suits you. The first meeting is free, and later visits are part of your fixed quote.

Yes. We build document-reading and email-drafting tools where a person approves anything that leaves the business, designed around UK GDPR. Where data is sensitive we can use a private model that keeps it in your control, and we work alongside your own compliance team.
