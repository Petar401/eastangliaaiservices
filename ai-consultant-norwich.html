Why a Norwich AI consultant?

Most AI advice is written for large companies in London. Norwich businesses are mostly small and medium firms where the owner still answers the phone, chases invoices and copies figures between systems. We work at that scale: one problem at a time, on-site when it helps, with someone you can actually meet.

Norwich City Council's economic plan describes a city centre that is increasingly a hub for new and early-stage businesses, with strength in fintech and digital services, alongside Norwich Research Park, the largest concentration of food and health research in Europe (Norwich City Council, 2025). That mix of professional services, research, retail, hospitality and trades is exactly where practical AI saves the most time.

Funding and free support in Norfolk

Norfolk businesses can often get help towards the cost of new technology. The New Anglia Growth Hub offers free, impartial business support. Norfolk County Council's Business Growth Fund offers grants of £5,000 to £50,000 towards up to 30% of eligible growth projects, and the Made Smarter programme supports SMEs adopting digital technology, including match-funded grants of up to £20,000 (Norfolk County Council, 2025; New Anglia Growth Hub, 2025). Eligibility and funding rounds change, so check with the Growth Hub first. We're happy to help you scope a project so it fits.

Frequently asked questions

Do you visit businesses in Norwich?

Yes. We are based in Eaton, Norwich, and work on-site across the city and the rest of Norfolk. The first discovery visit is free; any later visits are included in your fixed quote.

How much does an AI consultant in Norwich cost?

Our AI Opportunity Review starts from £395, team AI training from £595 for a half-day, workflow automation pilots from £1,500 and AI voice agents from £1,200 plus £149 a month. All prices exclude VAT and every project gets a fixed written quote first.

Are there grants for AI projects in Norfolk?
