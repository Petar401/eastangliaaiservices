Frequently Asked Questions

Is this AI Use Policy legal advice?

No. It's a free starting-point template generated from your answers to help you get moving quickly. We recommend having it reviewed by a qualified professional before you formally adopt it — every business's risk and regulatory position is different.

Is the AI Policy Generator really free?

Yes, completely free with no obligation. You'll see your full generated policy on screen immediately after answering 8 short questions. We ask for your contact details so we can send you the document and follow up if useful — nothing is sold to you as part of using the tool. See our Privacy Policy for full detail.

What's included in the generated policy?

A tailored document covering purpose and scope, definitions, approved AI tools, data handling rules, acceptable use, human review requirements, roles and responsibilities, UK data protection compliance, training, incident reporting and an annual review clause — around a dozen sections, shaped by your answers.

How long does it take?

About 3 minutes. Eight short questions about your business, your team, your data and the AI tools you use — no essay answers required.

Can I edit the policy after it's generated?

Yes — treat it as a first draft. Print or save it as a PDF, then adapt the wording to match how your business actually works before you roll it out to staff.

Does it cover UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act?

Yes, for UK-based businesses the policy references the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018 in the data handling and compliance sections. For businesses outside the UK it uses general wording instead, since specific laws vary by country.
