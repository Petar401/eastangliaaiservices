Surrounded by AI, still doing it by hand

Cambridge is one of the most intense science and technology clusters in Europe: Cambridge Ahead's Cluster Insights counts 25,872 companies with £53 billion turnover and 220,951 employees within 20 miles of the city (Cambridge Ahead, 2026), and Cambridge Network lists artificial intelligence among the area's internationally recognised strengths (Cambridge Network, n.d.). Cambridge Science Park, founded by Trinity College in 1970, is the oldest science park in the UK (Trinity College Cambridge, n.d.).

But most of the businesses that keep Cambridge running are not AI companies. The dental practice, the lettings agency, the electrician and the restaurant still answer every call, rekey every invoice and chase every booking by hand. That is the gap we fill. We take the kind of AI the labs build and apply it to the ordinary jobs that eat your week.

Local networks and support in Cambridgeshire

Cambridgeshire Chambers of Commerce, part of the British Chambers of Commerce network, is the main membership body for businesses across the county (Cambridgeshire Chambers of Commerce, n.d.). The Growth Hub run by the Cambridgeshire and Peterborough Combined Authority gives impartial business support across Cambridgeshire and Peterborough and some neighbouring districts, including West Suffolk and Uttlesford (Cambridgeshire and Peterborough Combined Authority, n.d.). Cambridge Network is another good way to meet firms across the cluster.

Frequently asked questions

Do you work with small businesses in Cambridge, not just tech companies?

Yes. We focus on ordinary businesses: clinics, lettings agents, trades, cafés and professional firms. The quickest wins are calls, bookings, invoices and common questions.

You're based in Norwich. How do on-site visits work in Cambridgeshire?

We book on-site days in advance and do the rest by video call and remote access. The first on-site discovery visit is free, wherever you are in the county.

Can you keep research or patient data private?

Yes. Every system is designed around UK GDPR, and for sensitive data we can run a private or locally hosted model so nothing goes to a public AI service.

Where can Cambridge businesses get free support with new technology?

The Cambridgeshire and Peterborough Growth Hub offers impartial business support, and Cambridgeshire Chambers of Commerce runs events and member services. We are happy to work alongside either.

Do you cover Peterborough, Ely and Huntingdon?

Yes. We work across Cambridgeshire, including Peterborough, Ely, Huntingdon, St Ives and St Neots, and nearby towns such as Newmarket and Royston.
