Surrounded by AI, still doing it by hand

Cambridge is one of the most intense science and technology clusters in Europe: Cambridge Ahead's Cluster Insights counts 25,872 companies with £53 billion turnover and 220,951 employees within 20 miles of the city (Cambridge Ahead, 2026), and Cambridge Network lists artificial intelligence among the area's internationally recognised strengths (Cambridge Network, n.d.).

But most of the businesses that keep Cambridge running are not AI companies. The dental practice, the lettings agency, the electrician and the restaurant still answer every call, rekey every invoice and chase every booking by hand. That is the gap we fill: the same kind of AI the labs build, applied to ordinary jobs that eat your week.
