How EAAIS began

EAAIS was created to bridge the gap between advanced AI tools and the everyday needs of East Anglia businesses. We started with one goal: make AI accessible, practical and profitable for local business.

What we're building toward

We're building a trusted AI partner for the region — assisting businesses automate work, improve efficiency, and unlock growth with solutions that actually fit their day-to-day operations.

Why East Anglia

East Anglia is home to ambitious businesses, but many still lack accessible AI support. Our local focus lets us deliver relevant, on-site and industry-aware solutions that larger firms often miss.

Who's behind it

EAAIS was founded in Norwich in 2026 by Petar Petkov, an AI automation consultant with a background in marketing, business management and more than three years of data administration across logistics, healthcare, retail and finance, and co-founded with Magdalena Peeva. We'd rather visit your office than send a deck, and we work hands-on with SMEs across Norfolk, Suffolk, Cambridgeshire and Essex.

What We Build

Our core
capabilities.

01

🧠

AI Agent Development

Custom AI agents — autonomous software workers that reason, plan, and act across your existing stack. We build them for East Anglia SMEs that want enterprise-grade automation without enterprise overhead.

Who it's for and an example

Best for

Sales teams, B2B agencies, recruiters & SMEs drowning in manual lead follow-up or repetitive research work.

  • AI agents that drive software end-to-end the way a person would.
  • For a B2B agency in Norwich: an outbound agent that researches prospects, drafts personalised emails, and updates the CRM nightly.
  • Outcome: 8–12 hours of sales-team admin returned per week.

Each agent is grounded in your own data, with guardrails, audit trails, and a hand-back-to-human path when confidence is low.

02

⚡

Workflow Automation

Workflow automation that connects the tools you already pay for — Xero, HubSpot, Slack, Sheets, Outlook — so the boring parts of operations run themselves.

Who it's for and an example

Best for

Finance, ops & admin-heavy teams in professional services, accountancy, logistics & e‑commerce wanting to cut manual processing costs.

  • Workflow automation that removes manual hand-offs between systems.
  • For an accountancy practice in Ipswich, Suffolk: supplier invoices arrive by email, get categorised, posted to Xero, and flagged for approval — without a human touching them.
  • Outcome: invoice processing time cut from ~6 minutes to under 30 seconds.

We design around your existing process first, not a vendor's template, so the automation fits how your team actually works.

03

🔮

Predictive Intelligence

Machine-learning models that forecast demand, flag anomalies, and surface opportunities — turning your historical data into decisions for next week, not next quarter.

Who it's for and an example

Best for

Retail, hospitality, manufacturing & distribution businesses planning stock, staffing, pricing or maintenance from real data.

  • AI automation that learns from your past to predict your near future.
  • For a multi-site retailer in Cambridgeshire: a daily forecast of footfall and till volume per store, used to set staffing for the week ahead.
  • Outcome: 15–25% reduction in over-staffing costs without dropping service levels.

Models are explainable — you see why a forecast moved — and we re-train as your business changes (covered by our AI Ops service).

04

💬

LLM Integration

Production deployments of large language models grounded in your own documents, with citations, guardrails, and a clean way to roll back when an answer is wrong.

Who it's for and an example

Best for

Customer support teams, legal & professional services, healthcare admin and any business handling high volumes of documents or tier‑1 enquiries.

  • A private LLM that answers questions from your knowledge base, not the open web.
  • For a professional-services firm in Chelmsford, Essex: a triage assistant that drafts replies to inbound client emails using prior case notes and current policy documents.
  • Outcome: first-response time on tier-1 enquiries down from ~2 hours to under 5 minutes.

We integrate Claude, GPT, or open-source models depending on data-residency needs — everything stays in your tenant when that matters.

05

🔗

Data Pipeline Architecture

Real-time data pipelines that collect, clean, and route information across every system you run — so dashboards, AI models, and reports all draw from one trusted source.

Who it's for and an example

Best for

Scaling SMEs, multi-site retailers, SaaS companies and operations teams stuck stitching spreadsheets between Stripe, Shopify, HubSpot, Xero, Sheets & BI tools.

  • The plumbing that makes AI automation reliable: clean data, in the right place, on time.
  • For a regional logistics firm in King's Lynn, Norfolk: vehicle telemetry, driver hours, and customer ETAs unified into a single live ops view.
  • Outcome: stale spreadsheet exports eliminated; ETA accuracy improved from ~70% to ~94%.

We design pipelines that are observable end-to-end — you'll know within minutes if a feed breaks, not the morning after a board report.

06

🛡️

AI Ops & Monitoring

Continuous observability for everything we (or anyone) deploys: drift detection, performance monitoring, cost tracking, and automated retraining when accuracy slips.

Who it's for and an example

Best for

Any business already running AI/ML in production that needs assurance models stay accurate, compliant and cost-controlled over time.

  • The maintenance layer that keeps AI automation accurate and on-budget.
  • For a manufacturer in Bury St Edmunds, Suffolk: alerts when a quality-control vision model starts mis-classifying parts after a packaging supplier change.
  • Outcome: model drift caught in hours rather than weeks, avoiding tens of thousands in scrap.

This is what separates an AI project that goes live from an AI capability that stays valuable two years on.

07

🎓

AI Training for Businesses

Hands-on AI training for your staff, built around the work they already do. We show teams how to get reliable results from tools like ChatGPT, Claude and Copilot, and how to keep company and customer data safe while they do it.

Who it's for and an example

Best for

Owners, managers and office teams whose staff already use AI informally, or who want to roll it out safely with a clear policy from day one.

  • Practical team workshops, leadership briefings and role-specific sessions, delivered on-site across East Anglia or remotely.
  • For a professional-services team in Colchester, Essex: a half-day workshop on drafting client letters and summarising case files, with clear rules on what data never goes into an AI tool.
  • Outcome: staff using approved tools with confidence, and a written AI use policy that people have actually read.

Every programme teaches people to check AI output as carefully as they write their prompts, so faster work doesn't mean less accurate work.

Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers,
no jargon.

What does East Anglia AI Services actually do?

We design, build and deploy custom AI agents, workflow automation, predictive intelligence and LLM integrations for small and medium businesses across Norfolk, Suffolk, Cambridgeshire and Essex — replacing manual, repetitive work with systems that run themselves.

How much does AI automation cost for a small business?

Most first projects start small. AI staff training starts from £595 for a half-day team workshop, an AI Opportunity Review from £395, a workflow automation pilot from £1,500, an AI voice agent from £1,200 plus £149 a month, and a custom AI agent or LLM integration from £3,000. Every project gets a fixed written quote after a free strategy call, so there are no surprise bills. See our pricing.

Which areas do you cover?

We work with businesses across Norfolk, Suffolk, Cambridgeshire and Essex, including Norwich, Ipswich, Cambridge, Colchester, Peterborough, Bury St Edmunds, Chelmsford and King's Lynn. On-site visits are available across the region, alongside fully remote delivery.

How is this different from buying off-the-shelf software?

Off-the-shelf tools make you adapt your business to their workflow. We build the system around how you already operate — connecting the tools you use today (Xero, HubSpot, Slack, Sheets, Outlook and more) — so the automation fits your business, not the other way round.

How long does a typical project take?

Most projects go from initial discovery to a live system in a matter of weeks, not months. Our process — discovery, architecture, build and deploy — is designed to get a working system in front of you fast, then refine it with real usage.

Do you offer ongoing support after deployment?

Yes. Every system we deploy is continuously monitored for accuracy, drift and cost, with alerts and automated retraining built in, and we remain on hand for support, changes and scaling as your business grows.

Do you offer AI training for staff?

Yes. We run hands-on AI training for businesses across Norfolk, Suffolk, Cambridgeshire and Essex, on-site or remotely: practical team workshops, short briefings for owners and managers, and sessions built around a single team's real tasks. Every programme covers how to check AI output and what data must never go into an AI tool. See our AI training page for details.
