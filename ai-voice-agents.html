Why missed calls matter

In a September 2026 survey of 1,500 UK small and medium-sized businesses, 67% said that on an average day they miss at least one call they believe loses them business, and the research estimates UK small businesses lose about £3.7 billion a year to unanswered calls (BT Business, 2026). That figure is modelled from owners' own estimates and the survey was commissioned by a company that sells its own AI receptionist, so treat it as a guide. Frequently asked questions

Does an AI voice agent sound robotic?

Modern voice agents sound natural and handle normal conversation, including interruptions. We tune the voice, script and pace to your business and test it on real call scenarios before it goes live.

Will callers know they are talking to AI?

Yes. We recommend the agent says it is an AI assistant at the start of the call. It is honest, it builds trust, and callers can ask for a person at any time.

Can it book appointments into my calendar?

Yes. The agent can check availability and book into your calendar or booking system, then send the caller a confirmation by SMS or email.

Which languages can it speak?

English as standard, and it can be set up to answer in other languages your customers use, such as Polish, Bulgarian or Romanian. We test each language before it goes live.

How much does an AI voice agent cost?

From £1,200 for set-up plus £149 a month, excluding VAT. The set-up covers discovery, the call script and knowledge base, calendar and CRM connections, testing and go-live. You get a fixed quote first.

What happens if it cannot answer a question?

It takes a message, or transfers the call to a person you choose for urgent or complex cases, and sends you a summary so nothing is lost.
