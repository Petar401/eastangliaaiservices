Privacy Policy

How we handle your personal data.

Last updated: May 2025 · East Anglia AI Services Ltd complies with the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

1. Who We Are

East Anglia AI Services Ltd is the data controller for information collected through this website and our services. Company No. 17381169 · Registered office: Amderley Drive, Norwich, NR4 6HZ. Contact: info@eastangliaaiservices.co.uk

2. What Data We Collect

  • Contact information (name, email, phone) when you enquire or book a call
  • Technical data (IP address, browser type, pages visited) via analytics
  • Communication data from emails or chat with ARIA
  • Business information you share during project scoping and delivery

Contact and report-request forms on this site are submitted via Web3Forms, a third-party form-processing service that relays your submission to us by email; Web3Forms acts as our data processor for this purpose.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To respond to enquiries and deliver agreed services
  • To send project updates and relevant communications
  • To improve our website and service quality
  • To comply with legal obligations

We will never sell your data to third parties.

4. ARIA Chatbot

The ARIA chatbot runs entirely in your browser as a local, rule-based assistant. No conversation content is sent to our servers, to Anthropic, or to any other third party. Chat history is stored only in your device's localStorage. Do not share sensitive personal data in chat.

5. Legal Basis for Processing

  • Contract: Processing necessary to deliver requested services
  • Legitimate interests: Improving our services and relevant communications
  • Consent: Marketing communications (withdraw at any time)
  • Legal obligation: Where required by UK law

6. Data Retention

Client data is retained for 7 years following project completion. Analytics data is retained for 26 months. You may request deletion at any time subject to legal obligations.

7. Your Rights (UK GDPR)

  • Right to access your personal data
  • Right to rectification of inaccurate data
  • Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten")
  • Right to restrict or object to processing
  • Right to data portability
  • Right to complain to the ICO (ico.org.uk)

8. Cookies

We set a small number of strictly-necessary items (your cookie choice, ARIA chat history, accessibility preferences) and, only after you click Accept, Google Analytics 4 cookies (_ga, _ga_F6TTH896D0) to measure aggregate traffic. Full details, retention periods and how to withdraw consent are in our Cookie Policy. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time via Cookie Settings in the footer.
